Our Corporate Heritage

Redefining Affordable Luxury in Nigeria

Nigson Properties Limited was established as the premier real estate and property management division of Nigson Group. We specialize in residential development, property sales, institutional project management, and comprehensive facility operations.

Our core commitment is delivering affordable luxury homes. We reject the notion that luxury must be prohibitive; instead, we combine modern architecture, precision craftsmanship, innovative building solutions, 24/7 solar independence, and robust security into homes that provide long-term investment value.
